4. Understand the Three Users

Our system will initially have three roles.

👨‍💼 Admin/HR

Admin can:

Add employees
Edit employees
Remove/deactivate employees
Manage departments
View attendance
Manage leaves
Manage payroll
Manage PF
View reports


👨‍💻 Employee

Employee can:

View profile
View attendance
Mark attendance
Apply for leave
View leave status
View salary
View PF details


👨‍💼 Manager

Manager can:

View team
View team attendance
Review leave requests
Approve/reject team leave
